# Money channel: locked brand decisions

Status: **locked** by the owner (colours). Everything else below is decided unless marked open.

## Colours (locked): "Black & Gold base + Daybreak orange"

| Colour | Hex | Job | Never use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#14213D` | Main background; ink for text on paper | - |
| Black | `#000000` | Data walls, dark story scenes (scams, crashes, secrets) | Small text on navy |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Paper (documents, cards, title sheets) | - |
| Light grey | `#E5E5E5` | Cards, desks, secondary paper, placeholder lines | - |
| **Gold** | `#FCA311` | **Brand**: key words, chapter stamps, dollar-amount tags, highlights, end screen | Losses; small handwritten text on white (use navy instead) |
| **Orange** | `#F35B04` | **Losses and alerts only**: crash lines, falling prices, shock numbers, warnings | Brand, titles, normal emphasis |

Rules:
1. Gold = money and the brand. Orange = danger or loss. They never swap.
2. Gold and orange never touch directly; orange always comes with a minus sign or a down arrow.
3. Gains are drawn in navy ink with an up arrow (no green: green is the reference channel's colour).
4. Thumbnails: navy or black background, white paper, one gold word and/or one orange shock number. At most these three accents.
5. Gold on white only for fills, big text, tags and highlights; small handwriting on white is navy.

## Look (decided)
- "Real paper" style: paper grain, rough scissor-cut edges, white paper rims, soft drop shadows; notebook, graph-paper, cardboard and chalkboard surfaces. No 3D.
- Studies of the reference style: `paper-billions-study.md`, `paper-billions-study-2.md`.

## Format (decided)
- 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 fps, 24 Mbps; max 10 minutes; US audience; money lessons + money stories.
