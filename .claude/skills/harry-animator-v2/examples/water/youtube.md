TITLE: What if all the water on Earth vanished?
ALT TITLES: All of Earth's water fits in a surprisingly small ball | What happens to Earth with no water at all?

DESCRIPTION:
If every drop of water on Earth disappeared, you wouldn't survive it, and the planet left behind would look very different.

Up to 60% of an adult's body is water, the oceans would leave basins averaging about 3.7 km deep, and the clouds would vanish along with water vapor, roughly half of Earth's greenhouse effect. Yet all that water, rolled into one ball, is under 1,400 km wide.

The basin drawing is not to scale; the Earth and water ball comparison is drawn to scale.
