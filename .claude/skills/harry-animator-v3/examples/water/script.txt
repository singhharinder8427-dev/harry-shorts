If every drop of water on Earth vanished, you wouldn't survive it. Up to sixty percent of an adult's body is water, so every person, animal and plant would instantly shrivel. The oceans would leave basins averaging about three point seven kilometers deep. Clouds would disappear. And water vapor is Earth's biggest heat trapper, about half the greenhouse effect. Without it, far more heat would escape into space every night, like a desert everywhere. Here's the strange part. Rolled into one ball, all that water would be under 1,400 kilometers wide.
