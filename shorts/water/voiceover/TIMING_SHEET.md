# Voice-over timing sheet: "What if all the water vanished?"

The video is built on **estimated** timing (4.7 syllables/s, a natural conversational pace). When your take arrives, I re-time the whole video to it, so you don't need to hit these numbers exactly. They are a guide for pace.

## Easiest: one single take

Paste the whole script below into ElevenLabs as **one** generation, export **MP3 (44.1 kHz, 128 kbps or higher)** and send it to me.

```
If every drop of water on Earth vanished, you wouldn't survive it. Up to sixty percent of an adult's body is water, so every person, animal and plant would instantly shrivel. The oceans would leave basins averaging about three point seven kilometers deep. Clouds would disappear. And water vapor is Earth's biggest heat trapper, about half the greenhouse effect. Without it, far more heat would escape into space every night, like a desert everywhere. Here's the strange part. Rolled into one ball, all that water would be under 1,400 kilometers wide. A planet nine times wider depends on it. Subscribe so you never miss the next what-if.
```

- Target length: about **44 s** of speech (about 37 s to 43 s is fine).
- Paste only the script: plain text, no tags, no notes like "speak slowly". A standard TTS model reads notes out loud.
- Write the number as `1,400`. Tell me whether the voice says "fourteen hundred" or "one thousand four hundred".
- The last line is the subscribe call to action. It should sound warm and direct, like the rest, not salesy. If the first take over-acts it, regenerate.
- Keep the words exactly as written. The captions and alignment assume the script is read as written.
