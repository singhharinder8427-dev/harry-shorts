TITLE: What if today was Earth's last day?
ALT TITLES: A 10 km rock is nine hours away. Can we stop it? | Could we stop the asteroid that ended the dinosaurs?

DESCRIPTION:
In this story, God has seen enough of our crime and corruption, and a 10 km rock is heading for Earth.

The rock falls at more than ten kilometres every second, about ten times faster than a bullet, so from the Moon's distance it would arrive in under nine hours. A rock that size once turned the ground to vapour at around 5,000 °C, wrapped the planet in dust within hours and sent giant waves to most coastlines in two days. It ended the dinosaurs. But in 2022, NASA crashed its DART spacecraft into the asteroid Dimorphos and shortened its orbit by about 32 minutes. Find a dangerous rock early, and a small push is enough.

The "punishment" is a story premise; the impact facts and the DART result are real. Telemetry numbers are a story clock based on 12 km/s; the space drawings are not to scale.
