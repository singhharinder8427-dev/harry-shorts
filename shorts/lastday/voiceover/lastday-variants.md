# Last day on Earth - five script variants (55 s)

## Variant 1

Imagine Earth's last day. Crime and corruption have grown so bad that, in this story, God decides humanity has gone too far, and throws a rock at us. It's ten kilometres wide, the size of the one that ended the dinosaurs. It moves more than ten kilometres every second, about ten times faster than a bullet. From the Moon's distance, it would arrive in under nine hours. When it hits, the rock and the ground below it turn to vapour, at around five thousand degrees. Within hours, dust and soot wrap around the whole planet. Within two days, giant waves reach most coastlines. So, could we stop it?
