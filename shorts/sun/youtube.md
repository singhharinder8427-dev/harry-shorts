TITLE: What if the Sun vanished right now?
ALT TITLES: If the Sun vanished, you wouldn't notice for 8 minutes | The Sun disappears. Earth keeps orbiting. Here's why

DESCRIPTION:
If the Sun vanished this second, the sky would stay bright for another 8 minutes and 20 seconds.

That is how long sunlight takes to reach Earth. Changes in gravity travel at the speed of light too, so Earth would keep circling the empty spot for the same 8 minutes 20 seconds, then leave in a straight line at about 30 km/s. Estimates put the average surface temperature below freezing within a week, and the oceans would ice over on top while staying liquid underneath for thousands of years.

Sizes and distances in the drawings are not to scale. The Sun cannot actually vanish; this is a thought experiment.
