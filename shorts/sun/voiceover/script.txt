If the Sun vanished right now, you wouldn't notice. Its light takes eight minutes and twenty seconds to reach us, so the Sun would keep shining in your sky. Earth would even keep circling the empty spot, because gravity travels at the speed of light too. Then the sky goes dark, and Earth flies off in a straight line at about thirty kilometers a second. Within a week, the average temperature would drop below freezing. The oceans would freeze over on top, but stay liquid underneath for thousands of years, warmed by Earth's own heat.
