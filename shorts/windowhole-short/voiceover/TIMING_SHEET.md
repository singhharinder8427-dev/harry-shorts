# Voice-over timing sheet: "Why does an airplane window have a tiny hole?"

The video is built on **estimated** timing (a natural conversational pace). When your take arrives, the whole video is re-timed to it, so you don't need to hit these numbers exactly; they are a guide for pace.

## Record one single take

Paste the whole script below into your voice tool as **one** generation (or record it yourself), export **MP3 or WAV (44.1/48 kHz)** and send it back.

```
Mia spots a tiny hole in her airplane window. Her seatmate Otto is sure it's a manufacturing mistake. It isn't. Up at cruising height, the air outside is too thin to breathe, so the cabin air is pressurized. That pressure pushes outward on every window, the whole flight. A window has several panes, and the hole is drilled in the middle one. It lets cabin pressure into the gap, so the outer pane carries the load. It also lets moisture escape, so the glass doesn't fog. Otto leans in, squints, and goes quiet. Mia smiles. Some mistakes are on purpose. Subscribe so you never miss the next strange fact.
```

- Target length: about **39 s** of speech. The whole Short must stay at or under 45 s.
- Paste only the script: plain text, no tags, no notes like "speak slowly". A text-to-speech model reads notes out loud.
- Keep the words exactly as written: the captions and the alignment assume the script is read as written. Numbers: say how the voice read them ("fourteen hundred" vs "one thousand four hundred").
- The last line is the subscribe line: warm and direct like the rest, not salesy. If a take over-acts, regenerate.

## Voice settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Voice | a normal, natural conversational voice (male or female), not a deep narrator, not a hype voice |
| Stability / consistency (if your tool has it) | medium (about 0.5) |
| Style / exaggeration (if your tool has it) | 0: more style over-acts and runs long |
| Speed | 1.0 to start; if the take runs long, `speed = take length / target length` |
