TITLE: Why does an airplane window have a tiny hole?
ALT TITLES: The tiny hole in your airplane window, explained | That hole in the airplane window is not a mistake

DESCRIPTION:
There is a tiny hole in your airplane window, and it is there on purpose.

Planes fly where the outside air is too thin to breathe, so the cabin is pressurized and pushes outward on every window. The small hole in the middle pane lets cabin pressure into the gap between the panes, so the outer pane carries the load. It also lets moisture escape, which helps keep the glass from fogging.
