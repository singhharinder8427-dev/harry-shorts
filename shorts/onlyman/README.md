# What if you were the only man left on Earth? (harry-animator v2)

Vertical 2D cartoon Short (designed 1080x1920, delivered 1440x2560 (2K), 60 fps, at most 45 s) drawn entirely in code
with the v2 toolkit (fake-3D perspective scenes, always-moving camera, whip transitions, costumed cast, story props,
one-word safe-area captions, continuous music bed, -14 LUFS master).

## Script (103 words incl. the subscribe line) - variant #3 "Ray & Maya"
`voiceover/script.txt`; recording guide `voiceover/TIMING_SHEET.md`.

## Shots
1. Sunny street (trees, sun, clouds, Ray's cat): every other man is gone - piles of empty shoes and hats; a crown pops on Ray.
2. Two-shot: sister Maya walks in, folds her arms, rolls her eyes.
3. New York: skyscrapers, a fire station, a dalmatian; a wall of 50 helmets, 49 pop away (about 1 in 50 left), label.
4. US road at dusk: power lines, a sparking streetlight; Maya grabs a toolbox + hard hat, fixes it, the lights pop on under moon and stars.
5. Rwanda: green hills, trees, birds, the PARLIAMENT; three women MPs wave; label.
6. Freezer of FROZEN SAMPLES opens in a cold mist; label.
7. Nursery at night (moon, stars): three baby boys in blue caps, the calendar flips JAN -> DEC.
8. Street again: Ray's crown slips off, the cat swats it away, Maya: "Good", walks off; subscribe; loops to frame 0.
