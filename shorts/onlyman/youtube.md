TITLE: What if you were the only man left on Earth?
ALT TITLES: The last man on Earth isn't as important as he thinks | One man, four billion women: what happens next?

DESCRIPTION:
Ray wakes up as the only man on Earth and thinks he's a big deal. His sister Maya has other plans.

If every other man vanished, about four billion people would be gone overnight. New York's fire department would lose almost everyone, since fewer than two percent of its firefighters are women, and about 97% of US electricians are men. But the world keeps going: Rwanda's parliament is already about 64% women, and sperm frozen for decades has still produced healthy babies, so new baby boys could arrive within about a year.

Cartoon story, simplified. Figures are the latest we could find (FDNY count from news reports; IWPR analysis of federal labor data; IPU 2024); "within about a year" is our estimate.
