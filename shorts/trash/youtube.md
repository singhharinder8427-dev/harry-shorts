TITLE: What happens to trash at a waste-to-energy plant?
ALT TITLES: How burning trash can make power and heat | Your trash doesn't vanish: here's one place it goes

DESCRIPTION:
Throw something away and it doesn't vanish. Here's how a waste-to-energy plant turns it into power, heat and road gravel.

A crane feeds trash into a closed furnace, where it tumbles down a moving grate and burns. The heat boils water into steam that spins a turbine for electricity, and some plants also send leftover heat to warm homes. Lime, activated carbon and fabric filter bags clean the smoke, the white plume is mostly water vapor, and magnets pull metal from the ash so the rest can be reused as road gravel in many countries.

Simplified cartoon, not to scale. Not every plant sends heat to homes, and rules for reusing ash differ by country.
