TITLE: The beach tree you should never sit under
ALT TITLES: Why this beach tree has a red band on its trunk | The manchineel: the tree that burns you in the rain

DESCRIPTION:
The manchineel looks like a shady apple tree, but its rain, its fruit, its leaves and its smoke can all hurt you.

Every part of the manchineel holds a milky sap that blisters skin, and rain can carry it onto anyone sheltering underneath. Its small green fruit tastes sweet at first, then burns the mouth and throat. In Spanish it is called the little apple of death, and smoke from its burning wood can inflame the eyes badly enough to cause temporary blindness.

The story is a cartoon; the castaway's reactions are exaggerated. Never touch or eat any part of this tree.
