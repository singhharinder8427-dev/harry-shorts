TITLE: Why mosquitoes pick you and ignore your friend
ALT TITLES: Why you are a mosquito magnet (and your friend isn't) | Mosquitoes have favourites: the skin science

DESCRIPTION:
Why does a mosquito pick you and ignore your friend? It comes down to your skin's chemistry, and which mosquito species happens to be flying.

In a 2022 study, volunteers with more fatty (carboxylic) acids on their skin attracted far more Aedes aegypti mosquitoes, and skin bacteria help turn those acids into each person's smell. A 2026 study tested three mosquito species on 119 volunteers and found that nobody attracted all three; each species preferred a different odour profile. Both studies are lab smell tests, and the 2026 results are correlations.

The story is a cartoon; the swarms and reactions are exaggerated.
