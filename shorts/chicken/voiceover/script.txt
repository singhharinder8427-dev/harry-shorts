Blob and his two neighbours lose everything overnight. Money, houses, fields. All gone. Three hungry days later, each of them gets one chicken. The first neighbour cooks his right away. Best dinner in days. Also his last. The second neighbour gets a hen and eats one egg every morning. Blob gets a rooster. No eggs at all. So he knocks on her door with a deal. Let my rooster meet your hen, and I'll share the eggs. She wanted eggs. Blob wanted chicks. Three weeks later, the first chicks hatch. About five months after that, they're laying eggs too. Blob's yard fills up so fast he can't keep up, so he hires the first neighbour and pays him in eggs. Same start. Same chicken. Now one is poor, one is comfortable, and one is rich.
