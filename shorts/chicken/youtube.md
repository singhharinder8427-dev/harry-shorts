TITLE: One chicken explains why the rich get richer
ALT TITLES: Same start, same chicken, three different lives | Why does the gap between rich and poor keep growing?

DESCRIPTION:
Three neighbours lose everything and each gets one chicken. Months later, one is poor, one is comfortable, one is rich.

The first neighbour eats his chicken right away. The second keeps a hen and lives on her eggs. Blob only has a rooster, so he pairs it with her hen and hatches chicks: eggs hatch in about three weeks, and young hens start laying at around five months. A flock that makes more chickens grows much faster than one hen making breakfast, and that's one reason wealth piles up where wealth already is.

This is a simplified story about one cause of inequality. Inheritance, luck, health and access to education matter too.
