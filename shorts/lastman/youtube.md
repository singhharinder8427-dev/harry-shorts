TITLE: What if you were the last person on Earth?
ALT TITLES: The real danger of being the last person alive | Last person on Earth: food isn't the problem

DESCRIPTION:
Ray wakes up and everyone else is gone. Free roads, free food... and then the lights go out.

With nobody running them, power plants shut themselves down, and one estimate says New York's subway tunnels would flood in about two days once the pumps stop. Canned food keeps its best quality for two to five years, so food isn't the problem. Loneliness is: being cut off from people raises the risk of early death about as much as smoking up to 15 cigarettes a day.

Cartoon story, simplified and not to scale. The two-day subway flood is a book estimate, not a measured timeline.
